Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Vesty (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a privacy-first tool for tracking the vesting and unlock schedules of crypto tokens, presale/IDO allocations, and company equity (RSUs). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Summary: Vesty has no user accounts. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see it. The app uses a few services to show market prices, deliver notifications and understand crashes and feature usage; none of them receive the amounts, wallets or schedules you enter.

Information you enter

You manually enter the data Vesty tracks, which may include:

  • Asset details (token/company name, ticker/symbol, network, amounts, contract address, claim website)
  • Vesting schedule details (dates, cliff, duration, frequency)
  • Optional cost basis and purchase/grant dates
  • Wallet or account names and optional blockchain addresses
  • Claim status and per-event reminder choices
  • App preferences (language, currency, notifications, biometric lock)

This information is never sent to us. We operate no account system and no server that stores your entries.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Your entries are stored locally using Apple’s on-device database (SwiftData) and app settings storage.
  • In your private iCloud (optional). If iCloud is enabled on your device, your data syncs through Apple’s CloudKit to your own private iCloud account so it stays available across your devices. This data lives in your iCloud and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Services the app uses

To work, the app communicates with a small number of services. None of them receive your amounts, cost basis, wallet addresses or schedules.

  • CoinGecko — to show live prices and your portfolio’s value, the app requests market data for the ticker symbols of the crypto assets you track (for example “ARB”), for the coins on your Markets watchlist, and for the text you type into the Markets coin search. Your display currency is sent with these requests. Quantities, holdings and amounts are never sent. From Vesty 1.2.0, company tickers from RSU grants are never sent. See the CoinGecko Privacy Policy.
  • alternative.me — the Markets tab fetches the public Crypto Fear & Greed Index. No personal data is sent.
  • CoinCap icon library — to display a crypto asset’s icon, the app may request the icon image for its ticker symbol from assets.coincap.io. From Vesty 1.2.0, RSU grants show a letter icon and make no request. No personal data is sent.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — the app registers for notifications, which involves a device push token being handled by Apple and Google/Firebase. It is used only to deliver app notifications.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) — the app fetches basic configuration, such as the minimum supported version. Standard device and app information is processed by Google to do this.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — if the app crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report (device model, iOS version, app version and the technical trace) is sent so we can fix it. It contains none of the data you enter.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — the app records anonymous usage events, such as the onboarding steps you reach, finishing onboarding, whether you allow notifications, opening the upgrade screen, a purchase completing or a schedule being added (with its asset type only), together with a random app-instance identifier, the app language and whether you use Vesty Pro. It helps us understand which features are used. It is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising and is not shared with data brokers.

Google’s handling of Firebase data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security page. Most reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device and require no server.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and the app contains no advertising SDKs.
  • We require no sign-up, email, or login.

Your control over your data

  • Edit or delete any entry at any time inside the app.
  • Settings → Delete all data permanently removes your entries from this device and, when iCloud sync is on, from your iCloud and every device signed in to it.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from the device.
  • You can disable notifications, iCloud sync, or biometric lock at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children

Vesty is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Not financial or tax advice

Vesty is an informational tracking tool. Dates, schedules, and any holding-period indicators are reminders only and are not investment, financial, or tax advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.
